Las Ranas

An indie Latin folk-rock band

“Blending borders, languages, and rhythms — we make music that feels like home on both sides.”

Lead Vocalist/Manager - Vanessa Martinez

Vanessa is the lead vocalist and manager of an indie Latin folk-rock band bridging the musical worlds of the U.S. and Mexico. With a commanding yet soulful voice, she channels the spirit of two cultures into every performance, blending heartfelt lyrics with rhythmic intensity. As the band’s manager, Vanessa guides their creative vision and cross-border collaborations, ensuring their sound stays authentic, grounded, and deeply connected to their roots.

Hobbies:

  • Working out
  • Traveling
  • Coding
  • Going to the cinema

Guitarist/Songwriter - Strummy McStrings

Strummy McStrings is the band’s guitarist and principal songwriter, known for crafting poetic lyrics and lush, rhythmic arrangements that define the group’s signature sound. With a flair for blending the storytelling traditions of Latin folk with the edge of indie rock, Strummy brings both humor and heart to every song. Whether strumming under desert stars or riffing in a crowded studio, his music captures the in-between spaces of two cultures, two languages, and one shared pulse.

Hobbies:

  • Cooking

  • Reading
  • Gardening

Bassist/Producer - Groove Mendoza

Groove Mendoza holds down the low end as the band’s bassist and doubles as their in-house producer. Known for his inventive bass lines and sharp ear for texture, Groove shapes the band’s sound from the ground up—balancing earthy folk roots with modern indie-rock polish. In the studio, he’s the quiet architect behind the band’s lush harmonies and layered rhythms, always chasing that perfect balance between warmth, grit, and groove.

Keyboardist/Mixing & Mastering Engineer - Luna Keys

Luna Keys is the band’s keyboardist and the sonic sculptor behind their polished sound. As the mixing and mastering engineer, she transforms raw recordings into immersive soundscapes that shimmer with emotion and clarity. On stage, her ethereal synths and melodic piano lines weave through the band’s folk-rock foundation, adding both depth and dreaminess. Behind the console, Luna’s meticulous ear ensures every track tells its story with balance, warmth, and soul.

Drummer/Recording Engineer - Boomy McBeat

Boomy McBeat is the band’s powerhouse drummer and resident recording engineer, known for turning raw rhythm into studio magic. With a natural sense of timing and an obsession for perfect tone, Boomy brings both explosive energy and technical precision to every track. On stage, his dynamic grooves drive the band’s heartbeat; in the studio, he captures that same pulse with an engineer’s precision, ensuring every snare crack and cymbal shimmer hits just right.
